> License record (PHOTO-PIPELINE §4). **Last updated:** 2026-11-18

Stock photos are stand-ins until HEAL's own photos arrive (PHOTO-PIPELINE §3). All are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial use, no permission or attribution required; you may not sell them unaltered or use them to build a competing image service. Only free-license photos were used (no Unsplash+).

The map (`public/photos/pmap.webp`) is built from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) tiles (ODbL); the credit "© OpenStreetMap contributors" is printed on the image and must stay. Rebuild with `node scripts/build-map.mjs`; the coordinates are 38.46074, -121.42004 (Nominatim for "8315 Grandstaff Dr, Sacramento").

Stock photos were fetched and cropped by `node scripts/fetch-stock-photos.mjs <empty dir> [portrait]`.

To replace a photo: drop the new file in `public/photos/` and update `src`, `alt`, `width`, `height` in `src/content/photos.ts`.
